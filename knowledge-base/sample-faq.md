# Knowledge Base — Sample FAQ

## Getting Started

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Visit the signup page, enter your email and a strong password, then confirm your email address. Setup takes under 2 minutes.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and PayPal. Bank transfers are available for annual Enterprise plans.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes. All new accounts start on a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card required.

## Billing

**Q: How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?**
A: Go to Settings → Billing → Change Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately (prorated). Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.

**Q: Where can I find my invoices?**
A: Invoices are available under Settings → Billing → Invoice History. You can download PDF copies or have them emailed to your billing address.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. Contact support with your order ID and a refund will be processed within 5–7 business days.

**Q: Why was my card declined?**
A: Common reasons include expired card, insufficient funds, or your bank blocking the transaction. Try updating your payment method under Settings → Billing, or contact your bank.

## Technical Issues

**Q: The app is loading slowly. What should I do?**
A: First, clear your browser cache and try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R). If the issue persists, try a different browser or disable extensions. Check our status page for any ongoing incidents.

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Click "Forgot password?" on the login page. Enter your email and you'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes. Go to Settings → Data → Export. You'll receive a download link via email within 24 hours containing all your data in JSON and CSV formats.

**Q: The integration with my CRM isn't syncing. How do I fix it?**
A: Try disconnecting and reconnecting the integration under Settings → Integrations. Ensure your CRM API key is still valid. If the issue persists, check that your CRM hasn't changed its OAuth scopes.

## Features

**Q: Does the product support team collaboration?**
A: Yes. You can invite unlimited teammates on Pro and Enterprise plans. Each user gets their own login, and you can assign roles: Admin, Member, or Viewer.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: iOS and Android apps are available in the App Store and Google Play. They support all core features. Offline mode is available for viewing data cached in the last sync.

**Q: Can I use the API?**
A: Yes. Full REST API access is included on Pro and Enterprise plans. API keys can be generated under Settings → Developer → API Keys. Rate limits: 1,000 requests/hour on Pro, 10,000 on Enterprise.
